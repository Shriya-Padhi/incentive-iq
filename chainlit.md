# IncentiveIQ

**Incentive assistant for An Electrification Company dealers.**

Pick a product, the customer's location and income tier in the ⚙️ settings, then ask about rebates and credits.

Every answer comes from official program pages and cites its source with the date it was fetched. If the indexed pages don't cover a question, IncentiveIQ says so instead of guessing.

*Products shown are fictional sample equipment. Program details change often; confirm on the official page before you buy.*
